The Numbers Don't Lie

Have you ever wondered how many clamps are sold annually? Well, you aren't alone. So we'll tell you. An astonishing 990 trillion clamps were sold in 2025. That's an average of 119,277 clamps for every person on planet earth!

Data courtesy The National Trust for the Preservation of Modern and Historical Clamps

Arts & Clamps

Is there anything more beautiful than a newly minted clamp? Thanks to their bold form and rugged usefulness, clamps have inspired countless works of art in every imaginable form. After all, as Pontiforque famously wrote, "If humanity had but a single muse...surely would it be the humble, mighty clamp!"

Excerpt from Sir Landry-Walls Pontiforque's Ode to a Clamp, #643

What if...?

Clamps exist. Everyone has used them. Everyone has loved them. Everyone has misplaced one when they really needed it and been quite put out. We all agree that we could not imagine life today without clamps. But what if we absolutely HAD TO imagine living without them? Where would civilization be without clamps?

That is not a question we will answer here.*
